Book a discovery call

Four phases, in order. You can stop after any one of them.

Every phase produces something you own and can use on its own. That is deliberate — an engagement that only pays off at the end is an engagement you cannot get out of.

01 — Diagnose

Find the real constraint

We look at how the organisation actually runs, not how the org chart says it does. Interviews, process walkthroughs and whatever data exists. The output is a written diagnosis of what is limiting growth, which is usually not what you were told it was.

What it involves

  • Interviews with the people doing the work, not only the people managing it
  • Walking a live job end to end, from first contact to money received
  • A look at whatever data exists, including the spreadsheets nobody mentions
  • A written diagnosis, circulated before anyone proposes a solution

How long

Typically two to four weeks

If you stop here

You have a written, evidenced view of what is limiting the business. That is a useful thing to own whether or not we do anything else together.

02 — Design

Build the target state

A specific description of how the process, the team or the reporting should work instead, costed and sequenced. Concrete enough that someone else could implement it. You can take this and run it yourself.

What it involves

  • A target-state design for the process, structure or reporting in question
  • Sequencing and dependencies, so the work runs in a survivable order
  • A cost and effort estimate honest enough to be argued with
  • Agreement on what success is measured by, before implementation starts

How long

Typically two to six weeks

If you stop here

You have a specification another party could implement. Some clients take this in-house, and that is a legitimate outcome rather than a lost sale.

03 — Implement

Do it with your team

We work alongside the people who will own it afterwards, rather than delivering to them. Slower in week one and considerably faster by week six, because nothing has to be handed over at the end.

What it involves

  • Working alongside the people who will own the process afterwards
  • Building in the open, so decisions are visible while they are reversible
  • Regular checkpoints against the measures agreed in phase two
  • Documentation written by your team, in their language

How long

Varies with scope — agreed up front, not open-ended

If you stop here

The change is live and running. Most engagements could end here, and some should.

04 — Embed

Make it stick

Measurement, documentation and the handful of habits that decide whether a change survives contact with a busy quarter. Most transformation work fails here, which is why it is a phase and not a closing email.

What it involves

  • Measurement that continues after we leave, owned by someone named
  • The management habits that decide whether a change survives a busy quarter
  • A review at a defined interval, agreed at the start
  • An honest assessment of what did not hold, and why

How long

A defined review period, not a retainer by default

If you stop here

This is the phase most transformation work skips, which is why so much of it quietly reverses within a year.

Ninety minutes. A straight answer about what is actually holding you back.

No charge, no deck, no obligation to engage us afterwards.
