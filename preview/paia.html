PAIA manual

Our manual under section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000, as amended by POPIA. It explains what records we hold and how to request access to them.

Draft: awaiting legal review. A PAIA manual is a statutory requirement with prescribed content and a prescribed request form. This draft has the right structure, but it is not compliant until the information officer, the registered address and the records schedule below are completed and an attorney has confirmed it.

4. Records held

A complete manual must list the categories of records we hold, and state which are automatically available and which require a request. The categories below are indicative and must be confirmed against what the business actually keeps.

  • Company records: incorporation documents, statutory registers, minutes and resolutions
  • Financial records: annual financial statements, tax returns, invoices, banking records
  • Client records: engagement agreements, correspondence, deliverables, working papers
  • Personnel records: contracts, payroll, statutory returns
  • Enquiry records: submissions through this website, as described in our privacy policy

[Confirm and complete this schedule]

5. Records available without a formal request

Nothing has been published under section 52 of PAIA. Our privacy policy, terms of use and this manual are freely available on this website.

6. How to request access

  1. Complete the prescribed request form (Form 2 of the PAIA Regulations). [Link to the form]
  2. Send it to the information officer at info@trevorgmenyatsogroup.co.za or the postal address above.
  3. Provide enough detail to identify the record and to identify yourself, and state the form of access you want.
  4. If the request is in the public interest or relates to your own personal information, say so and explain why.
  5. Pay the prescribed request fee where one applies. Access fees are payable before the record is released.

We will respond within 30 days. That period may be extended once, by up to 30 days, where the request is for a large number of records or requires a search through records held elsewhere. We will tell you in writing if that happens.

7. Grounds for refusal

We may refuse access where PAIA requires or permits it, including where release would unreasonably disclose someone else's personal information, breach a duty of confidence owed to a client, disclose commercial information belonging to a third party, or where the record is privileged in legal proceedings. If we refuse, we will tell you which ground we relied on and how to appeal.

This manual is available on this website, and on request at our registered address, at no charge. It is also lodged with the Information Regulator. [Confirm lodgement]
