Privacy policy

How we handle personal information, and what you can require of us under the Protection of Personal Information Act.

Draft — awaiting legal review. It states what the website actually does, but the retention periods, the information officer's details and the operator arrangements must be confirmed by Trevor and reviewed by an attorney before launch.

Who we are

TGM Group is the responsible party for the personal information described here. You can reach us at info@trevorgmenyatsogroup.co.za or 081 517 1016.

Information officer: [Name and contact details]. Under POPIA every responsible party must register an information officer with the Information Regulator. Confirm this is done and record the details here.

What we collect

We collect two kinds of information, and no more than that.

  • What you send us. If you complete the enquiry form or email us, we receive your name, work email address, organisation, the problem category you selected and anything you wrote in the message field.
  • What the website records. This site is static and carries no advertising trackers, no analytics cookies and no third-party scripts. Our host keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and availability purposes.

We do not use cookies for tracking. We do not build profiles. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone, under any circumstances.

Why we hold it, and for how long

We use what you send to respond to your enquiry, to prepare for a discovery call and, if we go on to work together, to run the engagement. That is the whole purpose.

Retention: enquiries that do not lead to work are kept for 24 months and then deleted. Records relating to an actual engagement are kept for the period South African law requires for business records — [confirm period with accountant] — and then deleted.

Who else sees it

Our enquiry form is processed by a third-party form service, and our email is hosted by a third-party provider. Both act as operators on our instruction and are bound to protect the information.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may, at any time and at no cost:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete it, where we are not required to keep it
  • Object to our processing it
  • Complain to the Information Regulator if you are not satisfied with how we responded

Email info@trevorgmenyatsogroup.co.za and we will respond within 30 days. Our PAIA manual sets out the formal access procedure.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information. This website is served over HTTPS only. If a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so on this page and update the date below.
