This file is a template. It never appears on the site, because `draft: true`
keeps it out of the index, out of the feed, out of the sitemap, and stops a page
being generated for it at all.

Leave it here. Copy it to a new filename when you write a real note, and set
`draft: false` on that copy only.

## Why this file exists at all

Two reasons. It gives you working frontmatter to copy rather than retype, and
it keeps the notes collection from being empty. An empty collection makes the
build print a warning on every page, which is noise that would hide a genuine
error later.

## Writing the real thing

Open with what happened, not with context. Short paragraphs. Specific numbers
where you have them, and a visible gap where you do not, the same as the rest of
the site.

A quote pulls out like this:

> The client did not ask for a dashboard. They asked why the report was late.

South African English throughout: organisation, optimise, programme, centre.
Money as `R12 500`. Dates as `14 November 2026`. No em dashes.

See `docs/writing-notes.md` for the full routine, including how to get the note
onto the live site.
