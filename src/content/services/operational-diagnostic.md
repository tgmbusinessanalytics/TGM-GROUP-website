Every engagement starts here. Before anything is built, someone has to find out
what is actually failing, where, and what it costs. That is the diagnostic.

Over three weeks we review the files and registers you use now, interview the
person who keeps them and the person who uses the reports, and examine the real
data. We look for two kinds of failure: work that is not being captured
properly, and work that is not being reported properly. The output is a written
findings document and a scoped build specification.

The diagnostic is R12,500, paid in full before work starts. If you
sign a build within 30 days of the handover, the full R12,500 is credited
against the build price. If the right answer is that you do not need a build, we
say so. The specification is yours either way.

This work starts in **TGM Business Systems** and looks across both divisions.
