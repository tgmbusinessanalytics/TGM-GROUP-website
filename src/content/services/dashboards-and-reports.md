If a client expects a report on the fifth of every month, someone is probably
spending the last days of the month assembling it. That is the work we remove.

Once the data is clean and in one place, we build dashboards on the tools you
already run, and set up the monthly client report so it is produced from the
data rather than assembled by hand. We agree the measures with you first. A
dashboard that shows activity instead of the thing you are accountable for is
decoration.

This follows data cleaning and consolidation, either as part of a Systems build
or as its own piece of work. It is not sold on top of data we have not seen.

This work sits with **TGM Business Analytics**.
