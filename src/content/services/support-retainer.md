A system that nobody looks after drifts. A site is added, a rule changes, a
report needs one more column, and within a year the system no longer matches
the business.

The retainer covers fixes, changes and support on a system we have delivered,
across either division. It is offered only after you have accepted the build and
is never bundled into the build price. It runs for six months at a minimum and
then month to month. Hours beyond the monthly five are quoted before the work
begins, and unused hours do not roll over.

This work sits with **TGM Business Systems**.
