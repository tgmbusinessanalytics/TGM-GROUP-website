A system either runs or it does not. We build the ones that run.

The build follows the specification from the diagnostic. For a contractor with
staff across several sites, that usually means attendance and timesheets
captured once at the site and reconciled without a spreadsheet, compliance files
and certificates tracked before an audit rather than during it, or job status
that anyone can see without phoning a supervisor.

We build on the tools you already run wherever we can, so there is no new
platform to learn and no house system to be locked into. The scope and the price
are fixed before work begins, and what acceptance looks like is written into the
agreement. If the work overruns inside the agreed scope, the overrun is ours.

This work sits with **TGM Business Systems**.
