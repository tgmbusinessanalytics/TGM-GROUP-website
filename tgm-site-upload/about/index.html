A transformation partner that stays past the diagnosis

TGM Group helps South African organisations improve how they operate, solve the problems underneath the symptoms, adopt AI and automation where it earns its cost, and grow in a way the business can absorb.

What we are

TGM Group is a business transformation and growth partner working with organisations in South Africa. The work divides into two practices — one focused on how a business runs, the other on what it can see about itself — and most engagements involve both.

The position is narrow on purpose. We are not a generalist agency and we do not sell technology. We work on the operating layer: the processes, the decision rights, the reporting and the data that determine whether a strategy turns into anything.

We also stay. The common failure of consulting is a well-argued recommendation delivered to a team with no capacity to implement it. Our engagement model has four phases and implementation is two of them.

Two practices, one group

Separate capabilities, one engagement. You deal with one team and one scope, not two suppliers coordinating with each other.
