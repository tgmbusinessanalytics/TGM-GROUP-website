6. How to request access

  1. Complete the prescribed request form, Form 2 of the PAIA Regulations: Request for Access to Record (PDF). A request on anything other than the prescribed form may be refused.
  2. Send it to the information officer at info@trevorgmenyatsogroup.co.za or the postal address above.
  3. Provide enough detail to identify the record and to identify yourself, and state the form of access you want.
  4. If the request is in the public interest or relates to your own personal information, say so and explain why.
  5. Pay the prescribed request fee where one applies. Access fees are payable before the record is released.

We will respond within 30 days. That period may be extended once, by up to 30 days, where the request is for a large number of records or requires a search through records held elsewhere. We will tell you in writing if that happens.

7. Grounds for refusal

We may refuse access where PAIA requires or permits it, including where release would unreasonably disclose someone else's personal information, breach a duty of confidence owed to a client, disclose commercial information belonging to a third party, or where the record is privileged in legal proceedings. If we refuse, we will tell you in writing which ground we relied on.

If you are unhappy with our decision, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator using Form 5, or apply to a court. You do not need our permission to do either. [Attorney to confirm the remedies available against a private body and whether an internal appeal applies]
