You do not have a growth problem. You have an operations problem.

Almost every organisation that comes to us arrives with a growth target and a plan to hit it. The plan is usually fine. What is missing is the operation underneath it — the process that breaks at volume, the reporting nobody trusts, the decision that waits three weeks for one person.

You cannot sell your way out of that. We find the constraint, design the fix with the people who will run it, and stay long enough to see whether it survives a busy quarter.

What we do

Five areas of work, delivered through two practices. Most engagements start in one and touch two — the constraint is rarely where the symptom is.

Business growth

Growth that the business can actually absorb — the right customers, a repeatable way of winning them, and an operation that does not buckle when it works.

Operational excellence

Find the constraint, redesign the process around it, and leave behind something the team runs without you. Fewer handoffs, fewer exceptions, less firefighting.

AI & automation

Automate the work that should never have been manual, and apply AI where it earns its cost. Assessed against your processes and your data, not against a vendor's demo.

Strategic execution

The distance between a strategy document and a changed organisation. Governance, sequencing, measurement and the discipline to stop the initiatives that are not working.

Leadership & capability

Build the management layer the organisation is about to need. Coaching, structure and the practical skills that let a founder stop being the escalation path for everything.

How an engagement runs

Four phases, in order. You can stop after any one of them.

01 — Diagnose

Find the real constraint

We look at how the organisation actually runs, not how the org chart says it does. Interviews, process walkthroughs and whatever data exists. The output is a written diagnosis of what is limiting growth, which is usually not what you were told it was.

02 — Design

Build the target state

A specific description of how the process, the team or the reporting should work instead, costed and sequenced. Concrete enough that someone else could implement it. You can take this and run it yourself.

03 — Implement

Do it with your team

We work alongside the people who will own it afterwards, rather than delivering to them. Slower in week one and considerably faster by week six, because nothing has to be handed over at the end.

04 — Embed

Make it stick

Measurement, documentation and the handful of habits that decide whether a change survives contact with a busy quarter. Most transformation work fails here, which is why it is a phase and not a closing email.

Evidence, not adjectives
